Privacy policy

Your data and Astro Backups

Effective date: 24 October 2026. This policy covers the personal Astro Backups Google Drive integration maintained by GitHub user DoV4-s.

Purpose and information accessed

The integration is used by its owner to store and recover encrypted backups. Once authorized, it accesses the Google Drive files and folders available under the granted permission, including backup file contents and metadata such as names, sizes, identifiers, modification times, and available checksums. It is intended to request the drive.file permission for files and folders created by the integration.

How information is used

Google Drive access is used to upload, organize, verify, download, and delete the owner's backup files. Google user data accessed through this integration is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Its use is limited to providing the backup and recovery functionality described here.

Storage and encryption

Archives are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload to Google Drive. The owner controls the local backup files, encryption secrets, and copies downloaded for recovery. Authorization tokens and connection settings are stored locally by rclone so that authorized backup jobs can run. Backup filenames, sizes, and other storage metadata may remain visible to the storage provider depending on the selected encryption method.

Sharing and service providers

Google receives the encrypted archives and related metadata to provide Drive storage. Backup information is not intentionally shared with other users or third parties by this integration. The owner can separately download or share their own files. Google's handling of stored information is governed by its applicable terms and privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls retention. Until an automatic retention policy is configured, backups remain until the owner removes them. Deleting a cloud backup does not delete local or downloaded copies. Deleted Drive files may remain in Drive's trash until permanently removed under Google's rules.

Control and revocation

The owner can disable backup jobs, remove local authorization settings, delete backup copies, or revoke the integration's access through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not by itself delete stored backups. Local and cloud copies must be managed separately.

This website

These information pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies set by the site itself. GitHub Pages hosts the pages and may process visitor information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and updates

Questions or requests can be raised through the public repository's issues. Do not post credentials or private backup data. This policy will be updated if the integration's data practices change.
