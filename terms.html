Using Astro Backups

Effective date: 24 October 2026.

Purpose and scope

Astro Backups is a personal, single-user integration maintained by DoV4-s. It is intended to store and recover encrypted backups in the owner's Google Drive. It is not offered as a hosted backup service for other users.

Authorized use

Use the integration only with accounts and data you own or have permission to manage. Google Drive access requires the account owner's authorization. The integration must be used in accordance with the applicable Google service terms and API policies.

Account and recovery responsibilities

The owner manages account access, local credentials, encryption secrets, storage capacity, backup schedules, and retention settings. Keep recovery secrets separately from the backed-up computer. Loss of the required passphrase or recovery key may make encrypted backups unrecoverable.

Availability and verification

The integration is maintained for personal use and has no service-level agreement or guaranteed recovery outcome. Backups can fail because of configuration errors, software changes, unavailable services, storage limits, hardware failures, or other causes. Check backup results and periodically test restoration before relying on a backup for recovery.

Privacy and third-party services

The privacy policy explains Google data access and backup handling. Google Drive, GitHub Pages, and rclone are separate services or software with their own applicable terms and policies. Astro Backups is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or GitHub.

Stopping use

The owner may stop using the integration at any time by disabling backup jobs and revoking Google authorization. Stored cloud backups, local archives, and downloaded copies must be deleted separately if no longer wanted.

Changes and contact

These terms may be updated to reflect changes to the personal integration. Questions can be raised through the public repository's issues. Do not include credentials or private backup information.
